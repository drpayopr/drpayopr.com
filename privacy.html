1. Identity and Responsibility for Personal Information

DRPAYOPR is a brand submitted for registration in Puerto Rico under the name of Dr. José Luis Santiago Castillo, who operates the brand as an individual and is responsible for handling personal information collected through drpayopr.com.

For privacy-related questions, please contact info@drpayopr.com.

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • The drpayopr.com website.
  • Professional development and service request forms.
  • Electronic communications related to our services.
  • Website analytics tools.
  • DRPAYOPR marketing communications and newsletters.

Our website is intended for adults, professionals, and organizations.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with DRPAYOPR, we may collect:

Contact information: name, email address, and other information voluntarily provided.

Professional information: organization name, institution type, location, professional development interests, and requested services.

Inquiry information: messages, specific needs, preferred language, and other information submitted through our forms.

Business information: information necessary to prepare proposals, coordinate services, and maintain records of professional activities.

Technical and website information: information about website interactions collected through Google Analytics, subject to applicable consent settings.

We ask visitors not to submit unnecessary sensitive personal information through our forms.

4. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  1. Respond to questions and service inquiries.
  2. Prepare proposals and coordinate professional services.
  3. Communicate about requested services and activities.
  4. Send marketing communications and newsletters in accordance with applicable requirements and recipient preferences.
  5. Maintain administrative and business records.
  6. Analyze website usage and improve its functionality.
  7. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those described in this policy without providing appropriate information or obtaining authorization when required.

Personal information may be accessed by authorized individuals involved in DRPAYOPR's operations, including:

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies or similar technologies to support certain functions and analyze visitor activity.

Visitors are provided with a mechanism to accept or reject nonessential cookies.

Where consent is required, nonessential analytics technologies should remain disabled until the visitor makes the applicable selection.

Visitors may also manage cookies through their browser settings.

9. Information Retention

DRPAYOPR retains inquiry information and related business records for up to five years unless a different period is necessary for legal obligations or legitimate business purposes.

Once information is no longer needed, it will be deleted or otherwise managed according to established retention procedures.

Retention periods are administered according to DRPAYOPR’s internal recordkeeping procedures.

10. Information Security

DRPAYOPR seeks to protect personal information through appropriate administrative and technical safeguards.

Although we seek to maintain appropriate security practices, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Individuals who have provided personal information may contact DRPAYOPR to:
