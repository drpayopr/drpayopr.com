# DRPAYOPR photo copy deterrents

Applied only to the About Dr. Payo portrait and the two team portraits (Héctor Santiago and Herminio Rodríguez).

- Original PNG files removed from this distribution and replaced by resized, compressed JPEGs. Keep full-resolution originals privately.
- Small © DRPAYOPR watermark baked into bottom corner of each JPEG.
- Transparent CSS overlay and scoped right-click/drag prevention. Image alt text remains intact.
- No global right-click or text-selection restrictions. LinkedIn controls remain outside photo overlays.

**Limitations:** These are deterrents, not security. Screenshots, browser developer tools, cached images, or direct URLs can still obtain public images. Watermarks can be cropped. Do not claim that saving is impossible.
