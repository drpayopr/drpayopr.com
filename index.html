About

Education grounded in experience, strategy, and collaboration.

Dr. Jose Santiago Castillo is an educator, author, and professional development facilitator with extensive experience in education, child care, leadership, and program development. His work focuses on helping educators and organizations strengthen collaboration, improve practice, and build systems that support children, families, and professionals.

His academic background includes graduate study in curriculum and teaching, with a professional focus on instructional practice, co-teaching, special education, leadership, and workforce development.
