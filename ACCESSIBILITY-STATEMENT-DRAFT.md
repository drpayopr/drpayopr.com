# DRPAYOPR accessibility statement — DRAFT, NOT FOR PUBLICATION

DRPAYOPR is working to improve the accessibility of its website and aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Accessibility testing and remediation are ongoing. We have **not yet verified conformance** with that standard and do not claim certification.

We welcome reports of barriers involving navigation, forms, language switching, documents, or other content. Contact us at **info@drpayopr.com** and describe the page, issue, and preferred way to receive a response. We will review accessibility requests and work to provide information in an accessible alternative format where feasible.

**Before publishing:** Confirm this contact channel is monitored, establish an internal response procedure, verify statements against completed testing, identify any known limitations accurately, and add an approval/publication date. Do not represent this draft as a certified accessibility statement.
